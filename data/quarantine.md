# Quarantine list

Every row is a real list with a reason checkable against the store or the paper cache, raised by one of the angles above (the reason names it), read by hand by the finder and re-read by an independent verifier told to refute it. The `verifier` column says keep or drop and `verifier_reason` (csv) gives the case; a dropped row stays on the list, since the issue asks for uncertain candidates to be flagged rather than dropped. `verdict` is empty on every row. `origin` is `pass2`.

Categories: **false-negative** (belongs to a deck the rule keeps it out of), **collateral** (a clean member a pass-one rule threw out), **hybrid** (straddles two decks), **different-deck**, **misassignment**, **data-anomaly**; only the first three occur. A store list prints in full with `select card, main, side from configurations where list_id = N` on `data/engine.duckdb`; a `melee:` id is a decklist_id in `data/raw-melee/`.

125 lists quarantined (114 store, 11 paper): 121 false-negative, 3 hybrid, 1 collateral. Confidence: 60 medium, 50 low, 15 high. Verifier: 104 keep, 20 drop, 1 none.
