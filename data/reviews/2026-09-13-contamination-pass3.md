# List ids were positional, and are the list's own from 2026-09-13

`list_id` was the position of a list in the raw cache, enumerated in filename order (`store.build`, `parse_cache`), which is a fact about the order files sort in and not about the list. The refresh run for issue #4 cached 64 new lists whose event slugs sort before most of the history, so every id from June onward shifted by about 32: pass two's row 18729 (Outsid3r, 24 June, Blink) became 18761, and 22651 (inf1nitus, 1 September, Tron) became 22683. 80 of the 125 pass-two ids and 46 pass-one ids came to point at a different list, and so did the ids quoted in `HEURISTICS.md`; `tracker/config.py`, contrary to the note this section replaces, quotes no list id at all. Nothing frozen under `data/tracking/` cites an id, and the archive check above was re-keyed on (date, event, pilot), under which every row lands as ruled.

Fixed the same day (issue #9). An id is now the event the list was published in and the pilot who registered it, which is what the site published: `modern-challenge-64-2026-07-0412846489#J4K3ST3R02`. One pilot can trophy twice in a league dump, so his second list takes an ordinal and his first keeps the bare key, and an id does not move when a later list arrives. The id column of all three archived reviews was re-keyed on (date, event, pilot), 486 ids in all, every one of which names its own row's date, event and pilot. The 86 pass-one rows played before the bans carry no id: the store starts at `config.HISTORY_START` and holds no list before it, so no id would reach them, and their date, event and pilot are the whole key.

Ids quoted inside the prose of the reviews, of `HEURISTICS.md` and of the rule comments were not re-keyed. Those written before the issue-4 refresh cannot be recovered, there being no record of the ordering they were taken from, and under the new scheme an integer id resolves to nothing at all rather than quietly to another list.

# Rulings

Alejandro ruled deck by deck on 2026-09-13: 66 rows in, 8 out. Every ruling is captured as a pilot entry in `HEURISTICS.md`, and each deck below carries a **Ruling** line saying which proposed rule was adopted, changed or refused. Four rulings cut across the decks:

- **A version is named by its markers, never by the splash line.** Tron's blue version is the deck on Stock Up or Force of Negation and its green version the deck on Chromatic Sphere, Chromatic Star or Sylvan Scrying; every other Tron list is colourless whatever it splashes, so a playset of Dress Down, Portent of Calamity, Malevolent Rumble or Ancient Stirrings inside the Eldrazi shell is a build reading and not a version change. Broodscale's Gruul version gains Lightning Bolt as a third marker and Blink's Esper half is read on Watery Grave or any blue spell. Measured: Tron blue 78 to 53, green 5 to 3, colourless 745 to 772; Broodscale gruul 455 to 457; Blink esper 575 to 577; no list changes deck. This reverses the pass-two reading of AlimTheBackpack's Brisbane list, which the splash line placed in green.
- **Control is the shell, not the card in a slot.** The UWr Control signature is Teferi with any one of Wrath of the Skies, Supreme Verdict, Terminus or Temporary Lockdown, control filling its sweeper slot to the meta the way it fills its interaction slot. Wan Shi Tong, Librarian, Stock Up and Brainsurge join the supporting tier, each already more common among members (15%, 14%, 17%) than Flow State and Thundertrap Trainer (10%, 3%), which the tier already named. An off-colour removal playset does not make a control list another deck while the rest of the mainboard stays under the splash line. Measured together: 32 lists in, 4 out, members 477 to 505.
- **Another deck's engine decides membership, and an engine is named by the card that runs it.** A `kethis` engine takes the two Kethis combo lists out of Grinding Station, a `namor` engine takes the mono-blue Charm and Shoal tempo shell out of Dimir Midrange, an `ephemerate` engine takes the four blink packages out of UWr Control, and the Oculus engine is read on Unearth rather than on Abhorrent Oculus itself, which admits dzk's 14th.
- **A deck ruled without a colour rule keeps none.** Izzet Prowess stays uncoloured: a Thoughtseize playset on an intact Izzet shell is a known innovation attempt, not a Grixis deck. For the record, the reason a colour rule on Prowess looks impossible is one card. Mutagenic Growth is green on record and castable through Phyrexian mana, and it alone accounts for 603 of the 605 members a splash line on blue and red would throw out.

# Quarantine list

Every row is a real list with a reason checkable against the store or the paper cache, raised by one or more of the angles above (the reason names them), read by hand by the finder and re-read by an independent verifier told to refute it. `verifier` says keep or drop and `verifier_reason` (csv) gives the case; a dropped row stays on the list, since the issue asks for uncertain candidates to be flagged rather than dropped, and most drops here are the verifier reading a ruling as already placing the list. `verdict` holds Alejandro's ruling on every row (2026-09-13): `in` admits or keeps the list, `out` leaves it outside, each with the reason in a few words. `origin` is `pass3`.

Categories: **false-negative** (belongs to a deck the rule keeps it out of), **different-deck** (a member that is another deck), **hybrid**, **version-misassignment** (a member whose version label is wrong or contested). A store list prints in full with `select card, main, side from configurations where list_id = '<id>'` on `data/engine.duckdb` (the id as of 2026-09-13 after the issue-4 refresh); a `melee:` id is a decklist_id in `data/raw-melee/`.

74 lists quarantined (64 store, 10 paper): 33 false-negative, 33 version-misassignment, 5 different-deck, 3 hybrid. Confidence: 58 medium, 13 low, 3 high. Verifier: 49 keep, 25 drop.
