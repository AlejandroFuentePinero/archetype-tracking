# Rulings

- **oko333, Jeskai Loki: out, a brew that is part of no deck.** Price of Freedom joins the Ponza line UWr Control excludes on. Measured: exactly this list moves, 0 store lists. Loki is not the line: JakeTMS's 2 July list and Grevv's Dallas list are Azorius Loki control with no land destruction and stay.
- **godseerker, Gruul Broodscale: in, the Forge is an innovation.** Mystic Forge is no longer an excluded card; the Karn, Mox Opal, Forge package the ruling named holds nothing in the history since the bans. Measured: exactly this list moves in, 0 store lists.
- **Kaoanchun, Izzet Affinity: in, Affinity without Explosives, an innovation worth flagging.** Explosives leaves the core and joins a supporting tier with Manufacturing and Krang, one of the three required. Measured: this list and Yojak3's 5 September league (Krang and Cranial Plating) move in, nothing leaves. Reading the two core cards alone would also admit ChewDawg's two Ravenous Robots lists and Costola91's two Frogmite lists, which stay outside. Affinity's frozen rows were recomputed for the one league list.

Names at this event are weak evidence: nine members carry a name that is a different deck outright (`W-U-B-G Blink` on the Goryo's shell, `Esper Broodscale` on the four Blink creatures, `Mono-Green Living End` on Trudge), and every one of them holds the whole 80% core of the deck the rule reads. The classifier's 330 members over 16 tracked decks are otherwise unremarkable.
