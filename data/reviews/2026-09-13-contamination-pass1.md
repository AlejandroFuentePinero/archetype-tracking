# Quarantine list

Member lists the membership rules admitted that look like a different deck, a hybrid, or a data anomaly, 
reviewed on 2026-09-13 against the example lists and each archetype's own consensus. Every list in the store 
and at the three paper events was scored; the flagged tail and the unflagged borderline were read list by list 
by one reviewer per deck, and every candidate row (this pass and the earlier one) was then re-read by an 
independent verifier told to refute it. Every row the earlier pass raised stays on the list; where the verifier 
argued against one, the `verifier` column says drop and its case is quoted under the deck. New candidates the 
verifier refuted are listed as dropped. The `verdict` column holds Alejandro's ruling where one has been given; a row 
ruled exclude has had the rule sharpened so the classifier no longer admits it (2026-09-13); a pre-ban row is out of 
the store altogether since the history now opens on the regime boundary. The rule sharpenings under each deck were 
the reviewers' proposals and the rule adopted is the one in tracker/config.py.

Categories: **different-deck** (another archetype under the rule), **hybrid** (straddles two decks), 
**data-anomaly** (parse or publication problem). Row detail in `2026-09-13-contamination-pass1.csv`, whose `origin` column says which 
pass raised the row (new, prior, both, added). A store list prints in full with 
`select card, main, side from configurations where list_id = N` on `data/engine.duckdb`; a `melee:` id is a 
decklist_id in `data/raw-melee/`.

## Jeskai Control (jeskai)

273 members, 33 flagged, 15 quarantined: 9 hybrid, 6 different-deck

Rule sharpenings proposed:

- Exclude on Wrenn and Six: measured 8 store members holding it (Parrotlet 5520, 459, 712, 1228, 3405 pre-ban; Kurusu 17257, Parrotlet 4076, Spezzagatti23 17654 post-ban) plus the melee Four-Color Control list; every one is a quarantine keep and no clean member holds the card. Preferred over Omnath, Locus of Creation, which I measured at 3 store members (17257, 4076, 17654) plus the melee list and leaves Parrotlet's five pre-ban Terminus lists inside.
- Exclude on Indomitable Creativity: measured 1 store member (DarthStone 22957); no clean member holds it, and the store has 74 unclassified Creativity lists that the shell cards could admit again.
- Exclude on Saheeli Rai (or Felidar Guardian, the same 3): measured 3 store members (Niko237 17669, 20240, 20317); no clean member holds either. Fable of the Mirror-Breaker measured at 4 (the same 3 plus 22957), so Saheeli is the sharper cut.
