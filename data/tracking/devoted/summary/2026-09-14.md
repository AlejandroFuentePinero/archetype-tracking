A big week for Devoted Combo on MTGO, and the fortnight under it rebuilt the sideboard.

- **MTGO** 6.5% of the top 32, from 4.9%, on 29 finishes in swiss-like tournaments. Far above the regime: the median since the Modern bans is 3.5 finishes, and 22 over the four weeks behind this one.
- **Conversion** over-converting, holding 8.0% of the top 8 against 6.5% of the top 32, on 9 top 8 finishes and 13 top 16 from 29 lists.
- **Build** in the fortnight to 20 September, across 83 lists and read against the fortnight to 6 September, four sideboard cards arrived at about half the deck: Pick Your Poison from none to 54%, Icetill Explorer from none to 48%, Doorkeeper Thrull from 6% to 52% and Reclamation Sage from 6% to 51%. Collector Ouphe fell from 72% to 34% in the same board, and Vexing Bauble moved to the mainboard in 19 lists.
- **Method** two readings changed on 21 September. A return row now counts distinct pilots rather than lists, two in either board, which withdrew 63 rows across the project from the frozen storylines, 1 of them this deck's. And a fortnight of fewer than ten lists can no longer set the MTGO bar that keeps a card off the paper watchlist. ADR 0008 carries both.
