Two Esper Blink pilots made the top 32 at Spotlight Dallas: evilgorrilaz 19th and littlesir 20th, both at 11-4.

On MTGO, Esper Blink achieved 44 finishes in swiss-like tournaments this week, 9.8% of every published top-32 slot, up from 7.5% the week before. That is its fourth straight week of more finishes.

Against its own history since the Modern bans it is at more than four times baseline, the median week being 10 finishes. It is a little above where it has just been: the four weeks behind this one ran a median of 30.5.

The Esper version is converting above its presence. 17 lists made top 8, 15.2% of that band against 9.6% of the top 32, and 26 made top 16, on 43 lists.
