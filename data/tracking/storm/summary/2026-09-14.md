A big week for Storm on MTGO. Its share climbed by two points, its volume ran to nearly twice both medians, and it is holding more of the top 8 than of the top 32.

- **MTGO** 5.4% of the top 32, from 3.2%, on 24 finishes in swiss-like tournaments. Well above its own history: the median since the Modern bans is 13 finishes, and 12.5 over the four weeks behind this one.
- **Conversion** over-converting, holding 8.0% of the top 8 against 5.4% of the top 32, on 9 top 8 finishes and 13 top 16 from 24 lists.
- **Build** in the fortnight to 20 September, across 59 lists, Veil of Summer climbed in the sideboard from 22% to 42% against the fortnight to 6 September, and Gandalf, Goblins' Bane appeared for the first time in 3 lists. Read against RC Baltimore, Grapeshot changed boards, 92% to 66% of mainboards and 8% to 34% of sideboards, and Orim's Chant fell in the sideboard from 67% to 29%.
- **Method** two readings changed on 21 September. A return row now counts distinct pilots rather than lists, two in either board, which withdrew 63 rows across the project from the frozen storylines, 8 of them this deck's. And a fortnight of fewer than ten lists can no longer set the MTGO bar that keeps a card off the paper watchlist. ADR 0008 carries both.
