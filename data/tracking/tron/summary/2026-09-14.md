Tron's volume climbed on MTGO, and the Colourless version is holding more of the top 8 than of the top 32.

- **MTGO** 3.8% of the top 32, from 2.7%, on 17 finishes in swiss-like tournaments. Level with its own history: the median since the Modern bans is 17.5 finishes, and 15 over the four weeks behind this one.
- **Conversion** the Colourless version is over-converting, holding 4.5% of the top 8 against 3.3% of the top 32, on 5 top 8 finishes and 7 top 16 from 15 lists.
- **Build** in the fortnight to 20 September, across 77 lists and read against the fortnight to 6 September, the deck swapped its top end: Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger climbed in the mainboard from 22% to 52% while Emrakul, the Promised End fell from 68% to 43%. The Ten Rings climbed in the sideboard from 13% to 48% and The Filigree Sylex from 47% to 70%.
- **Other versions** Blue 2 lists, 1 in a swiss-like tournament and 1 trophy; Green 1, 1 and none. MTGO only, and observability rather than a reading.
- **Method** two readings changed on 21 September. A return row now counts distinct pilots rather than lists, two in either board, which withdrew 63 rows across the project from the frozen storylines, 7 of them this deck's. And a fortnight of fewer than ten lists can no longer set the MTGO bar that keeps a card off the paper watchlist. ADR 0008 carries both.
