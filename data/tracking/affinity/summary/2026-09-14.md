Affinity's volume climbed on MTGO, its share up by two points on the week.

- **MTGO** 5.1% of the top 32, from 3.2%, on 23 finishes in swiss-like tournaments. Above where it has been: the median since the Modern bans is 21.5 finishes, and 15.5 over the four weeks behind this one.
- **Conversion** flat. 4.5% of the top 8 against 5.1% of the top 32, on 5 top 8 finishes and 11 top 16 from 23 lists.
- **Build** in the fortnight to 20 September, across 87 lists and read against the fortnight to 6 September, the sideboard came off its hate: Blood Moon fell from 71% to 46% and Swan Song from 71% to 51%, while Preordain fell in the mainboard from 72% to 46%. Seven cards printed return rows in the same fortnight, each held by two to four of the 87.
- **Method** two readings changed on 21 September. A return row now counts distinct pilots rather than lists, two in either board, which withdrew 63 rows across the project from the frozen storylines, 2 of them this deck's. And a fortnight of fewer than ten lists can no longer set the MTGO bar that keeps a card off the paper watchlist. ADR 0008 carries both.
