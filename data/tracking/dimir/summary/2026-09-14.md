A thin week for Dimir Midrange on MTGO, nine finishes and a share down by nearly a third.

- **MTGO** 2.0% of the top 32, from 2.9%, on 9 finishes in swiss-like tournaments. Level with its own history: the median since the Modern bans is 9.5 finishes, and 12 over the four weeks behind this one.
- **Conversion** flat. 0.9% of the top 8 against 2.0% of the top 32, on 1 top 8 finish and 4 top 16 from 9 lists.
- **Build** in the fortnight to 20 September, across 54 lists and read against the fortnight to 6 September, the deck rebuilt its card selection and its manabase at once. Preordain fell in the mainboard from 67% to 30% and Lórien Revealed from 27% to 2%, while Drown in the Loch climbed from 50% to 76%. Gloomlake Verge climbed from 42% to 72%, Bloodstained Mire from 31% to 61% and Scalding Tarn from 23% to 61%. In the sideboard Requiting Hex climbed from 28% to 70% and Stern Scolding from 17% to 69%.
- **Method** two readings changed on 21 September. A return row now counts distinct pilots rather than lists, two in either board, which withdrew 63 rows across the project from the frozen storylines, 4 of them this deck's. And a fortnight of fewer than ten lists can no longer set the MTGO bar that keeps a card off the paper watchlist. ADR 0008 carries both.
